# Executive research brief

## Decision
Prioritize employer conversations for an initial SE Bridge pilot while matching demand to actual provider capacity.

## Approach
Use a transparent employer fit score to screen prospects, then a separate scenario model for uptake, service hours, cost and illustrative absence value. Ground context in Statistics Canada data and employer/provider-verified inputs.

## Interpretation
The fit score explains relative fit under stated assumptions. It cannot establish that an employer will participate, employees will use the service, or absences will be prevented. Those questions require pilots.

## Pilot portfolio
1. Healthcare employer: test scale and workforce need.
2. Rural employer: test provider reachability as a gating condition.
3. Retail/frontline employer: test whether benefit eligibility assumptions understate potential demand.

## Immediate actions
Confirm shareable employer/provider data, validate eligibility and coverage, inspect Statistics Canada dimensions and periods, conduct discovery interviews, and compare forecast with pilot outcomes.
