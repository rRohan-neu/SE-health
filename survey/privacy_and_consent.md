# Canadian privacy and survey design checklist

This is a planning checklist, not legal advice or a compliance certification.

Before collecting responses:
- Identify the organization accountable for the collection and its purposes.
- Determine applicable laws based on the organization and activity: potentially PIPEDA, Ontario FIPPA / MFIPPA, or other statutes and contracts.
- Complete a proportionate privacy impact / risk assessment.
- Review vendor data residency, subprocessors, encryption, access controls, retention, deletion, breach notification, audit logs and contract terms.
- Confirm the actual survey configuration meets institutional requirements. "FOIP-compliant" is not a universal Canadian certification.
- Provide a clear privacy notice and collect only necessary organization-level information.
- Set retention and secure deletion dates before launch.

Safer defaults: no employee names, personal emails, health diagnoses, care-recipient details or home locations; aggregate worksite geography; restrict raw response access; suppress small cells.

Official starting points:
- https://www.ipc.on.ca/
- https://www.priv.gc.ca/
- https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90f31
- https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90m56
